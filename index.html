How StrangerChat Works

Pick text chat or video chat, add interests such as music, gaming, travel, coding, or movies, then connect with a random stranger. You can also skip filters and start a fully random chat.

If you are looking for a fast anonymous chat app, StrangerChat keeps the first step simple: choose a mode, match instantly, and leave or skip whenever you want.

Safety Controls for Anonymous Chat

Anonymous conversation works best when users stay in control. StrangerChat includes visible actions to stop searching, skip a match, leave a chat, or report a conversation.

Use a nickname, avoid sharing private details, and move on from any conversation that feels unsafe. These rules help make random chat friendlier for first-time and returning users.
